✨ What clipboard-manager Does for You

Every time you press Ctrl+C, Windows keeps only the last item on the clipboard. Copy a second thing and the first is gone. That is fine for simple work, and painful when you are moving phone numbers, addresses, snippets of text, links or passwords between documents all day long.

clipboard-manager quietly remembers everything you copy. When you need something you copied ten minutes ago — or yesterday — you open the app, type a few letters, and it is right there. No more switching back to a website just to copy the same sentence again.

🔎 Instant search

📌 Pins that stick

Pin the items you use every day — an email signature, a support code, a mailing address — so they never get pushed out.

💾 Survives restarts

Your history is saved to your own machine, so it is still there after a reboot instead of starting from zero.

🖱️ How You Use It Day to Day

There is nothing new to learn. You keep copying and pasting exactly as you always have. The difference is what happens behind the scenes.

  • Copy normally. Use Ctrl+C or right-click → Copy on any text, link or snippet. Every copy is captured automatically.
  • Open your history when you need something back. The app lives in the system tray, so it is always a click away without cluttering your taskbar.
  • Search instead of scrolling. Start typing what you remember about the item — a name, a few digits, part of a sentence — and the list narrows instantly.
  • Paste with a single click. Choose the item and it goes straight back onto your clipboard, ready to paste into whatever you were working on.
  • Pin the keepers. Anything you mark as pinned stays at the top of your list and is protected from being cleared out.

Because the window opens and closes in a fraction of a second, checking your history becomes a habit rather than a chore. Most people find they use it many times an hour without thinking about it.

🔐 Private by Design, Local by Default

Your clipboard often contains things you would never want uploaded anywhere: passwords pasted from a password manager, private notes, addresses, client details, one-time codes. That is exactly why clipboard-manager was built the way it was.

🧭 Troubleshooting Tips

The app does not seem to be running

Look for its icon in the system tray near the clock. Windows 11 sometimes hides new tray icons behind the small arrow — click that arrow to reveal them. You can drag the icon onto the visible tray area so it stays in view.

Windows showed a warning when I first ran it

Windows SmartScreen shows a caution message for many small, independently published programs that have not yet built up a download reputation. Choose the option to keep or run the file anyway if you downloaded it from the official project page linked above.

An item I copied is not in the list

Some password managers and banking apps deliberately block clipboard capture for security. That is expected behaviour and the app cannot override it. For everything else, normal copying should be captured right away.

I want to clear everything

Open the app and use the clear-history option to remove your stored clips. Since the data lives on your own machine, deleting your history removes it for good.
